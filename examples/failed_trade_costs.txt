2. With Safety Checks:
   - Checks run before trading: No gas cost
   - Failed checks: No gas wasted
   - Only attempts trades likely to succeed

Safety Checks Prevent:
✓ Trading tokens with no liquidity
✓ Trading when gas prices are too high
✓ Attempting trades with insufficient balance
✓ Getting stuck with unsellable tokens
✓ Network-related transaction failures
